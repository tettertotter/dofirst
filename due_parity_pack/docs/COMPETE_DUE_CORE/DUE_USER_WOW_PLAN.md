# Wow Plan for a Due User (Core, not bells & whistles)

## Keep what Due nails
- **Speed of capture**: todaypool opens at Quick Add; text or dictation; submit with partials.
- **Nagging that really nags**: repeating until acted upon; zero silent failures.
- **Effortless postpone**: the answer to “not now” is one tap (+10m, +1h, Tonight, Tomorrow AM).

## Make it better (without extras)
1) **Trusted‑people Today**  
   - Your partner can propose up to 3 items for your Today; each colleague up to 2. You accept or move—**no one assigns you a due date**.  
   - Reduces the “what matters today?” anxiety the moment you open the app.

2) **Cross‑platform**  
   - Same fast, respectful experience on **Android** and **Web**, not just Apple devices.

3) **Email-to-task that understands you**  
   - Subject → title; body → notes; `#tags`, `!1..5`, and `@today/@YYYY-MM-DD` inline parsing.

4) **Nagging 2.0**  
   - Smart cadence with quiet hours; small ramp from 5→10→15 minutes after first few nudges; configurable default.  
   - Action buttons in notifications: Done, +10m, +1h, Tomorrow AM.

5) **Sane defaults, fewer dials**  
   - Great default snooze cadence and postpone chips win 90% of cases. Advanced settings tucked away.

6) **Performance budget**  
   - Cold start ≤ 1.5 s; action-to-feedback < 100 ms; capture-to-schedule < 300 ms.

Success = a Due user feels at home in 30 seconds, and in a week says: *“It’s as fast, it nags just as reliably, and it actually helps me focus on today.”*
